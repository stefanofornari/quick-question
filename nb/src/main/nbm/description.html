QuickQuestion brings modern AI web chat interfaces directly into the NetBeans IDE. Open a lightweight window to chat with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, or Perplexity without leaving your coding workflow.

Ask quick questions even unrelated to your project for a quick, clean and spot-on answer, without polluting your context window.

Features:

  • Quick provider switching from a top toolbar
  • Persistent browser sessions across IDE restarts
  • Clean, distraction-free window inside NetBeans
